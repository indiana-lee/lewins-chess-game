Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LCG handles information in its current version.

Your privacy, at a glance

Information stored on your device

LCG stores your language choice, notification preference, and free-trial status locally on your device. Game play is kept on the device while you play.

No ads or tracking

LCG does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking in the current version. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Purchases

When you choose Premium, Apple processes the transaction and provides purchase entitlement information to LCG. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Feedback

If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address and message, to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Your choices

You can change app preferences in Settings. You can remove locally stored app data by deleting LCG from your device. Purchase management is available through your Apple Account.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as LCG changes. The latest version is available in the app.
