Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a game?

Open Lewin's Chess Game, choose your preferred board and game settings, then make the first move on the board.

Where can I change the language?

Open the app settings and select your preferred language. Lewin's Chess Game supports English, Chinese, German, French, and Spanish.

How do I restore Premium?

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then use the restore-purchase option in the Premium Membership screen.

Where is my game data stored?

Your game and app preferences are stored on your device. For more information, please read our Privacy Policy.
